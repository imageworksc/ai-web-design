Homepage First

We build the homepage before anything else, because it becomes the design system for the rest of the website. The messaging, colors, fonts, layout styles, buttons, spacing, and section patterns all get decided here — and every later page inherits them. Get the homepage right and the rest of the site stays consistent and moves faster. Skip this step and every page becomes its own argument.

Questions We Get Asked Every Week

Can AI build me a good website?

Yes — but the quality depends on who is using it. AI will produce a website quickly no matter who is at the keyboard. Whether that website is any good comes down to the team reviewing it, and what they refuse to let through.

How much does an AI website cost?

We work at a single team rate of $175 per hour rather than a package price, because we have no honest way to price your website before we understand it. A simple AI-assisted site can be built very efficiently. A highly customized one costs more because it takes more hours.

Will my site look like every other AI-built site?

Only if someone stops at the first output. That sameness is a symptom of nobody editing the result. Our design team takes the AI wireframe and pushes it until it looks like your business and not a template.

Do I have to build the whole website at once?

No. Many businesses launch with a strong one-page website and add pages later. Once the homepage and design system are established, additional pages move much faster.

What platform will my website be built on?

Smaller sites can deploy on a fast, modern AI-friendly stack. Larger sites with blogs, forms, integrations, and ongoing content management are better served by a CMS like WordPress or Drupal, so you can publish yourself without coming back to us for every edit.
